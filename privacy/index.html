See how your data is handled

Your media stays on your device. The app has no media-upload service.

What the app reads

When you choose folders, the browser gives the app temporary read access to those local files.

The app calculates SHA-256 hashes and opens two small edge samples from selected backup files.

It does not change or copy files. Sentinel does not save folder access for later sessions.

What is stored locally

IndexedDB stores up to three free checks or 30 Pro checks.

Each check stores folder labels, file names, paths, sizes, dates, hashes, and outcomes.

It does not store media bytes, EXIF fields, or location fields. JSON export and import let you move these records.

Licenses and network access

Buying or restoring Pro stores a license token and cached result in localStorage.

The app sends that token only to Sociobot for verification. After a response, it waits at least 24 hours before checking again.

Checkout runs on Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record.

Tracking

The app has no advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, cookies, third-party fonts, or runtime CDN scripts.

Its service worker requests only this app’s own files.

Your choices

Export your records before clearing browser site data if you want to keep them.

You can run checks without an account.

For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
