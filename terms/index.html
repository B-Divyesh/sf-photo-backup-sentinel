Sentinel verifies; it does not make backups. Keep your original files until you have independently tested a restore.

The service

Photo Backup Sentinel is a local browser utility that compares user-selected media files using content hashes and performs a sampled open/read check. A hash match is evidence of identical bytes at check time. A sampled read is not a complete playback, codec compatibility, media longevity, or future restore guarantee.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for choosing the intended source and destination, maintaining multiple independent copies, safely ejecting storage, and periodically restoring representative files. Do not delete originals solely because of a Sentinel result.
